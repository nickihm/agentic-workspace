# Daily

The planning files. See `Notes/AGENTS.md` for what each one holds.

- `YYYY-WN.md`: one to-do file per ISO week, created by `new-week-file`.
- `Long Term.md`: calendar of weeks for big dates and goals.
- `Top of Mind.md`: where everything stands, rebuilt by `determining-top-of-mind`.
- `Calendars.md` and `Important Email.md`: settings for the calendar, prioritizing, and email skills, created on first use.
- `Archive/`: past week files, Long Term rows, and Top of Mind backups, moved there by `archive-week` and `determining-top-of-mind`.
