# Archive

Anything finished or inactive that has no closer archive. Finished projects go to `Projects/Archive/`, inactive areas to `Areas/Archive/`, inactive accounts to `Accounts/Archive/`, and an account's finished projects to that account's `Archive/`. Read for history; do not add new entries.
