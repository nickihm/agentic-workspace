# Samples

Examples of the user's own writing. Agents read them to match the user's voice: greeting, sign-off, sentence length, and tone.

- `Emails/`: sent emails, read by `drafting-email`.
- `Slack/`: chat messages, read when drafting a Slack or Teams message.

Save each sample as `.md` or `.txt` with a short Title Case name. Remove other people's email addresses and anything private. Three to five per folder is enough. The `Sample ...` files are placeholders; replace them with the user's own.
